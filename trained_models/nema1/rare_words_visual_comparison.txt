## IMPACT ON MODEL LEARNING

### Without rare_words profile:
- Model sees "the" 320 times → becomes EXPERT at recognizing "the"
- Model sees "quixotic" 3 times → barely learns it exists

### With rare_words profile:
- Model sees "quixotic" 2,570 times → learns it VERY WELL
- Model sees "the" 0 times → relies on pre-existing knowledge from checkpoint

### Result:
- Dramatically better performance on technical terms
- Better handling of proper nouns
- More robust on uncommon vocabulary
- Slight degradation on very common words (acceptable trade-off)

User swipes: "kubernetes"
