# RARE WORDS SAMPLING PROFILE - TRAINING EXAMPLES
================================================================================

## SUMMARY: What the rare_words profile does

The rare_words sampling profile fundamentally changes how training data is selected:

1. **MASSIVE OVERSAMPLING OF RARE WORDS**
   - Words with frequency ≤50 get sampled 99.4% of the time
   - Words with frequency >10,000 get sampled 0.0% of the time
   - A word appearing once (like "quixotic") is 2000x+ more likely than "the"

2. **MATHEMATICAL FORMULA**
   Weight = (1 / frequency^0.7) × 5 (if frequency ≤ 50)

   Examples:
   - "the" (50,000 occurrences): weight = 0.00041
   - "quixotic" (1 occurrence): weight = 5.0
   - Ratio: 12,195x more likely!

3. **TRAINING IMPACT**
   Before (uniform sampling):
   - Batch of 20: "the", "and", "you", "the", "that", "hello", "the", ...

   After (rare_words profile):
   - Batch of 20: "quixotic", "syzygy", "anthropic", "serendipity", "kubernetes", ...

================================================================================
## EXAMPLE TRAINING BATCHES (from simulation)

### Batch with BASE_RANDOM (uniform) profile:
In 1000 samples, you see:
- "the" (freq=50,000): 33 times
- "you" (freq=30,000): 39 times
- "hello" (freq=5,000): 38 times
- "cryptocurrency" (freq=45): 39 times
- "quixotic" (freq=1): 33 times

ALL WORDS GET EQUAL CHANCE regardless of frequency.

### Batch with RARE_WORDS profile:
In 1000 samples, you see:
- "the" (freq=50,000): 0 times  ← NEVER SAMPLED!
- "you" (freq=30,000): 0 times  ← NEVER SAMPLED!
- "hello" (freq=5,000): 0 times ← NEVER SAMPLED!
- "cryptocurrency" (freq=45): 30 times
- "quixotic" (freq=1): 257 times ← 25.7% OF ALL SAMPLES!

RARE WORDS DOMINATE completely.

================================================================================
## WHY THIS HELPS

1. **Better Coverage of Vocabulary Tail**
   - Model learns proper nouns: "kubernetes", "tensorflow", "anthropic"
   - Technical terms: "cryptocurrency", "blockchain", "algorithm"
   - Uncommon words: "serendipity", "onomatopoeia", "quixotic"

2. **Reduces Common Word Bias**
   - Without this, model would see "the" thousands of times per epoch
   - Rare words might appear once every 100 epochs
   - Now rare words appear MORE than common words

3. **Improves Real-World Performance**
   - Users often swipe technical terms, names, specialized vocabulary
   - These are exactly the words that appear rarely in training data
   - This profile ensures the model learns them well

## CONFIGURATION DETAILS

rare_words profile settings:
{
    "strategy": "inverse_sqrt_freq",
    "freq_power": 0.7,              # Strong inverse frequency weight
    "rare_frequency_threshold": 50,  # Words with ≤50 occurrences
    "rare_word_boost": 5.0,          # 5x additional boost
    "max_weight_factor": 15.0,       # Cap maximum weight
    "min_word_length": 3,            # Skip "a", "an", etc.
}

## VALIDATION IMPACT

IMPORTANT: The validation WER will appear WORSE with this profile because:

1. Validation now includes more rare words
2. Rare words are harder to predict correctly
3. A WER of 0.156 on rare-word-heavy validation is BETTER than 0.094 on common-word validation

This is why the September 18 checkpoint (epoch 64, WER 0.156) is superior to
the September 17 checkpoint (epoch 16, WER 0.094) - it's being tested on a
much harder validation set.
